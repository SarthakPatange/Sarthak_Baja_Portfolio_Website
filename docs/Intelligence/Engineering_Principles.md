# Engineering Principles

Version: 1.0

Status: Repository Constitution

Purpose

Every engineering decision,
every design decision,
every interaction,
every animation,
every paragraph,

must satisfy these principles.

If a feature violates one,

it should be redesigned.

Not polished.

Redesigned.

---

# Principle 1

Engineering First

Visitors come to understand engineering.

Not web design.

Engineering always remains the primary product.

---

# Principle 2

Evidence Before Claims

Every statement should eventually be supported by

CAD

Simulation

Manufacturing

Testing

Competition

Reflection

Evidence builds trust.

---

# Principle 3

Understanding Before Complexity

Reveal

Overview

↓

Subsystem

↓

Detail

↓

Evidence

↓

Reflection

Never begin with complexity.

---

# Principle 4

Motion Must Teach

Every animation explains.

Nothing moves without purpose.

---

# Principle 5

Typography Leads

Typography introduces.

Engineering demonstrates.

Images validate.

---

# Principle 6

One Question Per Viewport

Every viewport answers one engineering question.

Nothing more.

---

# Principle 7

Every Decision Has Context

Never publish isolated numbers.

Explain

Compared to what?

Measured how?

Validated where?

---

# Principle 8

Document Trade-Offs

Real engineering involves compromise.

Mass

↓

Cost

↓

Strength

↓

Manufacturability

↓

Reliability

Document those compromises honestly.

---

# Principle 9

Engineering Honesty

If validation does not exist,

say so.

If assumptions exist,

state them.

Transparency creates credibility.

---

# Principle 10

Reflection Completes Engineering

Every project ends with

What succeeded

What failed

What changed

What would be done differently

Engineering maturity is demonstrated through reflection.
