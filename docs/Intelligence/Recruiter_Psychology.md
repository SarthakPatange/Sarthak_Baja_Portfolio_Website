# Recruiter Psychology

A recruiter spends seconds forming an opinion.

Within

5 seconds

they judge professionalism.

Within

20 seconds

they judge engineering depth.

Within

60 seconds

they decide whether to continue.

The portfolio must therefore answer

Who is this engineer?

What problems can they solve?

How do they think?

Can they communicate?

Would I trust them with my product?

Every interaction should reinforce those answers.
